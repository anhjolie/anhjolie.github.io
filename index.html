Hello, I’m Thanh Huyen — but everyone calls me Jolie. I’m a retoucher, photographer and art director working across fashion, jewelry and beauty. I love the moment an image stops being “almost there” and starts to feel inevitable: the right light on a gemstone, the fall of a silk hem, the colour that holds a whole campaign together.

My work spans brand campaigns, lookbooks, editorial stills, runway-show visuals and social design. Whatever the format, I care about the same things — a clear idea, careful execution, and images that feel unmistakably like the brand they belong to.

I’ve been lucky to collaborate with jewelers, fashion labels, beauty brands and designers, and I’m always keen to take on the next story worth telling.
